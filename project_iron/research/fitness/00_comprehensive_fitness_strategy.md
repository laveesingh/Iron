## High-Level Strategy: The Core Pillars

### 1. Mechanisms of Muscle Growth
Muscle hypertrophy is not a singular event but a biological adaptation to specific forms of stress. The consensus in 2025 narrows down the drivers of growth to three primary factors, with one reigning supreme.
*   **Mechanical Tension (The Primary Driver):** The force generated by muscle fibers to resist a load. This triggers the **mTORC1 pathway**.
*   **Metabolic Stress (The Secondary Driver):** The accumulation of metabolites (lactate, H+) which increases motor unit recruitment.
*   **Muscle Damage (The Overrated Driver):** Excessive damage inhibits growth because resources are diverted to repair rather than building.

### 2. Training Architecture
*   **Volume Landmarks:** Train between your **MEV** (Minimum Effective Volume) and **MRV** (Maximum Recoverable Volume).
*   **Intensity:** Train close to failure (**0-3 RIR**) to ensure high-threshold motor units are recruited.
*   **Frequency:** Hit muscles **2x per week** to align with the protein synthesis window (24-48 hours).

### 3. Nutritional Logic
*   **Protein:** **1.6-2.2g per kg** of bodyweight. This is the non-negotiable building block.
*   **Carbs:** Fuel high-intensity performance. Cycle them based on activity (High on leg days, Low on rest days).
*   **Fats:** Essential for hormonal health. Never drop below 0.6g/kg long-term.

### 4. Metabolic Health
*   **Fat Loss:** Is a multi-step process (Mobilization -> Transport -> Oxidation). You must be in a deficit to oxidize the mobilized fat.
*   **Adaptation:** The body fights weight loss by lowering NEAT and downregulating thyroid/leptin. **Refeeds and Diet Breaks** are the antidote.

### 5. Recovery First
*   **Sleep:** 7-9 hours. Deep sleep releases Growth Hormone; REM sleep consolidates motor learning.
*   **Stress:** Chronic stress (cortisol) is catabolic. Manage your "Allostatic Load."
