# Fitness Plan v1 (Base Building, Low DOMS, ACL-Safe Return to Run)

## Intent
Build a strong “base” without getting crushed by DOMS or overuse injuries, while rebuilding running endurance after ACL surgery (1 year post-op). This plan is designed to be **progressive**, **knee-friendly**, and **simple to execute in Bangalore**.

This plan is optimized for:
- Strength and muscle retention
- Aerobic base (Zone 2)
- Fat loss support (without appetite rebound and burnout)
- Knee/leg durability (tendon + calf + quad capacity)

## Core Principles
- **Start submaximal (RIR 3-4)** for 2–3 weeks, then gradually push to **RIR 2**.
- **Low-to-moderate volume**: you should finish sessions feeling “trained” not “wrecked.”
- **Cardio is mostly Zone 2**, plus a conservative run/walk progression.
- **Pain rules**: knee pain \(0–3/10\) is okay; \(4–5/10\) means reduce volume; \(6+/10\) stop/regress.

---

## Weekly Structure (Weeks 1–4)
**Target:** build consistency and tissue tolerance.

- **Mon**: Strength A (Full Body, knee-friendly)
- **Tue**: Zone 2 (bike / incline walk) 25–35 min + Knee prehab 10 min
- **Wed**: Strength B (Full Body)
- **Thu**: Rest or easy walk + mobility (10 min)
- **Fri**: Strength A (repeat, slightly lighter than Monday) OR Zone 2 25–35 min if legs are tired
- **Sat**: Run/Walk (ACL-safe) 20–30 min (very easy)
- **Sun**: Rest + weekly review (5–10 min)

If 3 lifting days feels like too much initially, do **2 lifting days** in Week 1 and add the 3rd in Week 2–3.

---

## Strength Workouts (Plan v1)
### Session Rules
- **Warm-up**: 5 min light cardio + 5 min dynamic mobility + ramp sets.
- **Main sets**: stay at **RIR 3–4** for Weeks 1–2, then **RIR 2–3** Weeks 3–4.
- **No grinders**. Perfect reps only.

### Strength A (Full Body)
- Box Squat or Goblet Squat: 3×6–8 (knee-friendly depth)
- Dumbbell Bench Press: 3×8–10
- Romanian Deadlift: 3×8–10
- Cable Row / Dumbbell Row: 3×10–12
- Seated Calf Raise: 3×10–15
- Core: Plank 3×45–60s

### Strength B (Full Body)
- Leg Press (controlled): 3×10–12
- Overhead Press (standing or seated): 3×8–10
- Hamstring Curl (seated if possible): 3×10–12
- Lat Pulldown / Assisted Pull-ups: 3×8–12
- Lateral Raises: 3×12–20
- Core: Pallof Press 3×10/side

---

## Knee Prehab (10–12 min, 3–6x/week)
Pick 3–4 movements:
- Terminal Knee Extensions (TKE): 2×15–20
- Split Squat ISO (hold): 2×30–45s/side (pain-free range)
- Tibialis Raises: 2×15–25
- Soleus Raises (bent-knee): 2×12–20
- Step-downs (slow eccentric): 2×8/side

---

## Running (ACL-safe Return to Run)
### Weeks 1–2
- 2–3 sessions/week
- **Run/Walk 20–25 min**:
  - 1 min jog / 2 min walk × 7–8
- Pace: “could hold a conversation.”

### Weeks 3–4
- 2–3 sessions/week
- **Run/Walk 25–30 min**:
  - 2 min jog / 2 min walk × 6–7

### The only progression rule
Increase only **one** variable per week:
- total minutes OR
- jog interval length OR
- frequency

---

## Zone 2 Cardio (Fat loss + heart/lung base)
Prefer **cycling** or **incline walking** early to reduce joint stress.

Goal: 2 sessions/week × 25–45 min.

---

## Diet (Simple, Bangalore-friendly)
### Keep it boring and repeatable
Focus on a few “default” meals you can get reliably:

#### Protein anchors (pick 1–2 per meal)
- Eggs (boiled/omelette)
- Chicken (grilled/curry with controlled oil)
- Fish (when available)
- Paneer / tofu
- Curd / Greek-style curd (hung curd works)
- Dal + rice (combine with curd/eggs to raise protein)
- Soya chunks (cheap, high protein)

#### Simple plate rule
- **Protein**: 1 palm-sized portion (or 3–5 eggs)
- **Carbs**: 1 fist (rice/roti/idli/dosa)
- **Veg**: 2 fists (sabzi/salad)
- **Fat**: 1 thumb (ghee/oil/nuts)

### Tracking (keep it minimal)
- Log **protein grams** and **steps** in the app daily.
- If cutting: keep “late-night random snacks” out of the house.

---

## Goals in the App (how to use it)
In `Daily Tracker`:
- Fill **Steps**, **Protein (grams)**, **Sleep**, **Knee Pain**.
- Check off **Mobility** and **Knee Prehab**.
- Log sessions in **Weekly Activities**:
  - Strength Session (Plan v1)
  - Zone 2 Cardio (minutes)
  - Run/Walk (minutes)

The `Dashboard` will automatically compute weekly totals and rolling averages.

---

## Progression (Weeks 5–8)
If Weeks 1–4 felt stable (knee pain stable <= 3/10, no major DOMS, recovery good):
- Keep strength at 3x/week but increase RIR target from 3–4 to 2–3.
- Increase Zone 2 to 3 sessions/week (optional).
- Transition run/walk toward continuous easy jogging (only if pain-free).

---

## Red Flags (Regress Immediately)
- Knee pain rises week-to-week
- Swelling, instability, sharp pain
- Achilles pain that worsens in the morning
- Shin pain that becomes localized and sharp
- Sleep quality crashes for 3+ nights while training feels heavier

If these occur: reduce running volume by 30–50%, switch to cycling Zone 2, and keep knee prehab consistent.
