# Comprehensive Smoking Cessation Strategy: Master Strategy (L1)

## 1. Executive Summary
This document serves as the master blueprint for a comprehensive smoking cessation strategy. It integrates pharmacological, behavioral, technological, and environmental interventions into a cohesive system. The objective is to maximize the probability of successful cessation by attacking the addiction on multiple fronts: the chemical dependence (Hardware), the habit loops (Software), and the physical context (Environment).

The 2025 approach moves beyond "willpower" and leverages:
1.  **Precision Pharmacology:** Matching drugs (Varenicline, Cytisine) to metabolic profiles.
2.  **Cognitive Rewiring:** Using CBT and Mindfulness to dismantle the "need" belief.
3.  **Environmental Design:** Reducing friction for healthy choices and increasing friction for smoking.
4.  **Digital Scaffolding:** Using technology to externalize executive function.

## 2. System Architecture
The strategy is built on six interconnected pillars. Each pillar addresses a specific failure point in the cessation process.

*   **Foundation:** **Pharmacological Support** quiets the physiological noise, allowing **Behavioral Frameworks** to rewrite the habit loops.
*   **Container:** **Lifestyle & Environmental Design** creates a physical context where smoking is difficult and unnecessary.
*   **Execution:** **Strategic Approaches** (Taper vs. Cold Turkey) define the specific tactical plan.
*   **Support:** **Technological Interventions** provide real-time feedback and accountability.
*   **Frontier:** **Emerging & Alternative Areas** offer novel interventions for resistant cases.
